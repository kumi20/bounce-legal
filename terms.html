Free and Premium content

World 1, the daily challenge and its streak, and playing levels shared by others are free. Bounce Premium unlocks every other world including each new monthly world, creating and publishing levels in the editor, the daily leaderboard and premium skins.

Purchase options

  • Premium Monthly: auto-renewing subscription, 1 month, 9.99 PLN per month.
  • Premium Yearly: auto-renewing subscription, 1 year, 49.99 PLN per year.
  • Premium Lifetime: one-time purchase, 89.99 PLN, including future worlds.
  • Prices are shown in the App Store in the currency of your country.

Subscription terms

  • New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the subscription starts and payment is charged to your Apple ID.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription anytime in your Apple ID account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).
  • Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple according to its policies.

Shared levels

Levels you publish must not contain offensive, hateful or illegal content, including in their names. Players can report levels; reported levels may be hidden and removed. Players can block authors on their device.

Game progress

Progress is stored on your device and synced through your iCloud when available. Purchases can be restored with "Restore Purchases" in the app.
