Bounce Ball: Retro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Bounce Ball: Retro ("the app") is a game developed by Jakub Kumięga. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

Data we collect

The developer does not sell or share personal data, and the app has no developer accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only data the developer can see is the content you choose to publish (see "Shared levels").

Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

Your game progress (stars, best times, unlocked worlds, skins and your daily streak) is saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the same progress is synced between your devices through your private iCloud storage, which only you can access. A small summary of your streak is shared with the app's home screen widget on the same device.

Shared levels

If you publish a level from the level editor, the level, the name you give it and a random iCloud identifier of its author are stored in the app's public iCloud database, so that anyone with its code can play it. Reports you send about a shared level are stored the same way. Published levels are public; do not put personal information in level names. You can ask for a level to be removed by opening an issue (see Contact).

Downloaded content

The app checks once a day for new or updated level files published on GitHub Pages (kumi20.github.io). Like any website, GitHub may log the request, including your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement. No personal data is sent.

Apple services

  • Game Center – if you are signed in, your best times and achievements are submitted to Apple's Game Center. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • iCloud – progress sync and shared levels use Apple's iCloud under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • In-App Purchases – Bounce Premium is processed entirely by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. Publishing levels requires an iCloud account and is part of Bounce Premium.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions and removal requests can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/kumi20/bounce-legal/issues.
